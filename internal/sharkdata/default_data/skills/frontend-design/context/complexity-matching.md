# Complexity Matching Standard

A reusable standard for matching implementation effort to the aesthetic vision.
Elegance comes from executing the chosen vision well — not from a fixed level of
intensity.

## The Standard

**Match implementation complexity to the aesthetic direction.**

- **Maximalist directions** need elaborate code: extensive animation, layered
  effects, rich textures, and orchestrated motion. Under-building a maximalist
  vision makes it look unfinished.

- **Minimalist or refined directions** need restraint and precision: careful
  attention to spacing, typography, and subtle detail. Over-building a
  minimalist vision makes it look noisy and undermines the direction.

## Why It Matters

Bold maximalism and refined minimalism both succeed. The failure mode is
mismatch — spending elaborate effort where restraint was called for, or cutting
corners where richness was the whole point. Decide what the committed direction
actually demands, then invest exactly that much.
