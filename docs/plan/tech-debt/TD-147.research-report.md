# TD-147 research: I-08 comparison time and cost deltas

## Scope and vocabulary

TD-147 concerns paired comparison deltas for elapsed time and provider cost,
and their relationship to published noise bands. It is distinct from run-level
time/cost measurement and from the existing quality comparison field.

## Ubiquitous vocabulary

A *paired delta* is a producer-owned signed comparison between declared left
and right evaluations. A *noise band* is an absolute observed variation range,
not a rule for choosing comparison direction. A time/cost delta needs both
defined before it can be reported.

## Findings

The comparison record carries `quality_delta` only; it contains no
`time_delta` or `cost_delta` ([compare-lifecycle-evaluations.sh](../../../bench/scripts/compare-lifecycle-evaluations.sh)).
The live comparator currently produces even `quality_delta` as null, so there
is no hidden time/cost calculation available to reuse. F10's report renders
the schema-owned upstream-gap reason and explicitly forbids subtracting two
evaluations locally ([report-lifecycle.sh](../../../bench/scripts/report-lifecycle.sh)).

TC-088 locks this boundary down: its time/cost classification path is driven
by a hand-built aggregate because production emits no paired delta, and both
time and cost must render the named upstream-contract gap rather than bare
`unavailable` ([tc088_dimension_separation_and_noise_band_test.sh](../../../bench/scripts/tests/tc088_dimension_separation_and_noise_band_test.sh)).

## Dependency and cross-boundary impact

A correct producer contract must specify the comparison pair, delta direction,
units, eligibility, and how a signed paired delta is compared to the existing
absolute-value noise band. Those choices cross the comparator, I-08 schema,
F10 aggregate/report contract, and UAT. Deriving them in `report-lifecycle.sh`
would create an unowned statistic and conflict with REQ-F-016.

## Alternatives

1. Preserve the named gap — correct behavior while AC-011 remains staged.
2. Extend the comparison record with producer-owned `time_delta` and
   `cost_delta`, plus a declared pairing/direction/noise-band rule — viable
   after an owner decision.
3. Subtract raw evaluation totals in F10 — rejected: comparison identity and
   statistical semantics are absent, and the current contract forbids it.

## Decisions

Research passes: TD-147 is a genuine upstream comparison-contract gap, not a
reporting implementation defect. Advance it to `triaged`; retain the explicit
placeholder until an F09/I-08 comparison-contract owner decision defines
paired delta and noise-band semantics. This decision is independent of
TD-146's F08/I-07 per-gate-attribution decision. Do not mark it resolved or
`wont_fix`.
