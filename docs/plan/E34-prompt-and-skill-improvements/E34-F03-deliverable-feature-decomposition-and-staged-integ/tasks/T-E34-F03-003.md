# Preserve UAT gate integrity and activation closure

## Goal

Make bundled UAT policy distinguish independent assessment from owner decisions
while keeping live-path, security, and unresolved activation obligations blocking.

## Scope

- Files to modify: UAT `SKILL.md`, `redteam-rubric.md`, `uat-agent.md`, and `interaction_prompts_test.go`.
- Files to create: none.

## Acceptance Criteria

- See `spec.md` AC-003 through AC-005 and REQ-F-003 through REQ-F-005.
- AC-T1: Assessor verdict, owner decision, conditions, and demonstrability disposition remain distinct reported facts.
- AC-T2: A declared `contract-only` edge never waives missing live wiring, authentication, authorization, integrity, unsafe exposure, or a current AC.
- AC-T3: Internal activation closure requires caller-chain, shared-contract, production-path, and wiring-removal evidence.
