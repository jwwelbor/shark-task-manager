# Shark Attack Skill and Role Protocol

This feature packages the reusable `shark-attack` skill and project setup guidance for a chair-led council, declarative role roster, durable council memory, inbox-style communication, escalation review, and workflow-role-aware self-pull without granting roster members Shark mutation authority. It is the primary E38 deliverable: coordination is expressed as prompts and Markdown procedures, while Shark remains the source of workflow state, claims, leases, filtering, and dispatch prompts. Dependencies: none; execution order: 1; size: 3 (M). Produces the role and communication contract consumed by E38-F06 and E38-F07, and consumes X-03 from E19 for role-aware pull/claim plus X-05 from E32 for embedded Shark-data and override distribution.
