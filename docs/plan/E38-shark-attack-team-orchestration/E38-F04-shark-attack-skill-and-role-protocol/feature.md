# Shark Attack Skill and Role Protocol

This feature packages the reusable `shark-attack` skill and project setup guidance for a chair-led council, declarative role roster, durable council memory, inbox-style communication, escalation review, and workflow-role-aware self-pull without granting roster members Shark mutation authority. It is needed to make team coordination repeatable across refreshed workers and projects, and supplies the role/communication contract consumed by scheduling, aggregation, and reporting. Dependencies: E38-F01; execution order: 2; size: 3 (M). Produces: I-04 to E38-F02, E38-F03, and E38-F05, using architecture §4.5. Produces: X-03 for E19 role-aware pull/claim and X-05 for E32 embedded Shark-data and override distribution.
