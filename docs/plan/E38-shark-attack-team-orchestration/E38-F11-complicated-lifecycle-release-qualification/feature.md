Shark Attack v2. It did not exercise a complicated hierarchy, cross-feature

independent review, QA, UAT, or literal approval.

feature. Fresh native workers must make and integrate nontrivial production

survive a lost response and interrupted council responder, and complete the
project's real quality and acceptance gates.
