# Lightweight Handoff and Operator Guidance

This feature documents the operator-facing handoff produced by the Rider procedure: what was pulled, claimed, dispatched, completed, blocked, escalated, or left for the next pass. It uses ordinary Shark status, claim, note, and history commands plus bounded Markdown artifacts; it does not add preview/start/resume/summary commands, a ledger, or a reporting service. Dependencies: E38-F04, E38-F06, and E38-F07; execution order: 4; size: 2 (S). It produces concise guidance for human operators and X-04-compatible evidence for existing telemetry rather than a new telemetry surface.
