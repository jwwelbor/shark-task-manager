# B088 repair plan: impact-based review findings

## Diagnosis and approved decision constraints

At the declared baseline, the review contract admits preference-only Nits without an identified consequence, and a report-only Nit can still prevent a clean PASS. The consolidator and craft workflow also require five scores per changed file and attach triage/blocker thresholds to those scores, allowing style preferences to create work independently of a defect. The behavior spans native, dispatched, comparable-CLI and shared craft producers, plus Python packet validation and Go review/QA admission. A display-only edit would leave those producers and consumers inconsistent.

Apply Tech Director decision 4279 (B088 note 4279) and decision 4276 (B085 note 4276): omit new preference-only findings and stop using per-file scores as a finding source; retain a finding only when it identifies an effect on a user, behavior, invariant, documented standard or maintenance task, with evidence and consequence. Preserve the explanation and raw evidence when a previously reported blocker is refuted and withdrawn. Under the B085 report-only policy, a blocker downgraded to a stylistic Nit records the original concern, evidence and disposition, then ends triage with zero edits and zero tracker writes. Substantive surviving findings continue through normal triage.

The B085 review/coverage defect (finding 4087) is owned by B085 and is not broadened into this plan. B088 must preserve compatibility with its candidate-bound decisions and accepted packets. E34 notes 3961 (unattended triage) and 3962 (diff-aware coverage reuse) are separate proposals and are not authorization to change those contracts here. B088 decision 4279 supersedes the unresolved Nit prerequisite recorded in notes 4267 and 4269.

## Approach and preserved behavior

1. Define impact-based admission and severity in the shared review craft contract: every retained finding must identify the affected user/caller, behavior, invariant, documented standard or maintenance operation; state the consequence; and cite evidence. A mere naming preference with no such effect is not a finding.
2. Remove the Nit category and per-file numeric scoring as sources of findings from all producers: native workflow, six dispatched angle prompts and consolidator, comparable CLI prompts/writer, shared bug/change prompts, feature prompt and alternate report skeleton. Preserve substantive standards citations, risk hotspots, caller chains, tests evidence, blocker/actionable non-blocker handling and compact clean reports. Incomplete review coverage remains explicitly INCOMPLETE.
3. Keep raw findings and candidate identity bound through consolidation. A refuted blocker retained only as stylistic preference must preserve its original statement, refuting evidence and report-only disposition, with no source edit or tracker filing. Do not silently drop evidence or call an unrefuted claim refuted.
4. Preserve existing packet hashes, accepted packets and historical decision records. Define a contract/version signal for new packets before tightening Python or Go readers. Readers continue to accept historical packets under their prior rules; new packets must enforce the impact contract consistently at review and QA admission.
5. Preserve stable finding fingerprints and candidate-bound disposition matching, including line-shift reuse for actionable findings. Do not alter B085's coverage/identity obligations or the independent E34-3961/3962 scopes.
