## Runtime model catalog discovery

Assert hidden-only pins, incomplete or malformed later pages, unsupported effort, missing catalog and ambiguous default all fail before adapter start. Capture catalog source, CLI version, selected metadata and digest without a synthetic secret or credential.

Observed protocol evidence: local `codex-cli 0.171.0` generated `ModelListParams` with a continuation cursor and `ModelListResponse` with `data` plus `nextCursor`. Model entries expose `model`, `displayName`, `description`, `hidden`, `isDefault`, `defaultReasoningEffort` and `supportedReasoningEfforts`. A read-only local request returned 10 visible entries, including the eight picker choices supplied by the owner plus two additional catalog entries. Model identifiers are omitted from this file.

The app-server catalog may be bundled or cached and does not establish entitlement. No `/v1/models` query, token-file read or inference request was made in this investigation.
