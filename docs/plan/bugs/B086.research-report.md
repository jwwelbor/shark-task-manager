# B086 research report

## Scope

B086 concerns the host's disposition of a surviving, actionable review finding when nobody can confirm an interactive triage proposal. A review verdict describes the candidate. A finding disposition accounts for what happened to the work. “Fix” needs evidence on the repaired candidate. “Defer” needs a reason and a retrievable owner or work item. The parent owns workflow transitions. E34 note 3961 proposes evaluating finding weight, stakes, changed lines and loop budget. It supplies no approved numerical thresholds.

## Decisions

1. **Extend the existing host triage path.** Apply scope and severity first. An accepted obligation or unresolved blocker stays with the active repair and follows its rework or escalation route. Nits retain their existing report-only treatment. For eligible non-blockers, prefer Fix when the change is local, low stakes and verifiable within the remaining budget. Consider the proposed fix's changed lines and whether they overlap the candidate. Total diff size alone gives no estimate of repair cost. The current triage skill's approximate 20-line example is guidance, not an approved cutoff.
2. **Require an observable result.** For Fix, record the fingerprint, repair and verification against the resulting candidate. For Defer, record the fingerprint, reason and verified key of an existing or newly created item of the correct type. Search again on retry, including after an ambiguous create result. Preserve accepted dispositions unless new evidence changes them. This keeps partial batch completion recoverable.
3. **Escalate when a safe outcome cannot be established.** Missing decision inputs, a refused write, unresolved ownership or exhausted budget on mandatory work should surface to the parent with the affected fingerprint and reason. Use project retry limits and evidence of recurrence. Never infer recurrence solely from the review round. Keep the read-only reviewer's decisions distinct from subsequent host actions so resolving a finding cannot silently rewrite the original evidence.
4. **Alternatives and open design decisions.** Automatically filing all non-blockers as debt reintroduces B066. Applying interactive confirmation universally reproduces B086. Granting all callers unconditional create privileges would change attended triage. The repair plan should define the host's unattended signal, source of remaining budget, permitted writes and deterministic tie-breakers, then exercise both modes, blocked writes, duplicate retries, budget exhaustion and re-verification after a fix. These are policy choices. The causal branch is explicit in the current contract.
