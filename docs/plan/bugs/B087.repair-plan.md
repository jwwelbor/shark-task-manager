## Repair design

Resolve its artifact hashes against an independently retained, parent-verified receipt. Never select by newest filename. Where a caller cannot establish that provenance, use full review. Any rejection after the selected PASS and before the current review disqualifies that receipt, even when its files and angle inputs still match. An unavailable or unverifiable history requires full fresh review. For each claimed credit, apply the same parent-owned eligibility check during current review admission and historical QA replay. On replay, use the selected predecessor and the accepted successor review as the interval endpoints. The QA operation has its own identity. Read the interval independently of the successor’s declaration.
2. Compute the prior-tree to current-tree delta in the same frozen source context as the full baseline diff. Keep the complete successor delta, including paths restored to baseline. Resolve impact before selecting obligations in the current gate manifest. Compare each proposed credit with the inspection required by the successor’s independently approved snapshot. A material change to that obligation requires fresh inspection, even when the predecessor receipt remains valid for its own approval. Check lineage, path, blob, file kind and mode, plan/specification and rubric inputs (including rendered includes, angle definitions and standards). Record the inputs an angle actually consulted and the boundaries its judgment covers. For a bounded first implementation, permit carry only for file-local obligations with an explicit closed set of verifiable inputs. Review cross-file obligations fresh on every successor. Dynamic lookup, unresolved linkage, changed or unbounded decision context, or inability to establish independence requires full fresh inspection of the entire current manifest with zero carry whenever the extent of impact cannot be established. A hash match alone never proves independence.
3. Keep one versioned coverage section in the existing angle evidence. Bind each carried unit to the prior receipt and fresh angle, source path and identity, obligation, input identities and current impact decision. Each current angle reports its own fresh work. Reconcile fresh and eligible carried units against the complete manifest and rubric. Migrate readers before enabling partial dispatch. Legacy complete six-angle reports remain admissible under existing checks and require the additional provenance to fund partial coverage. For comparable CLI, admission and QA require the producing attempt’s own candidate-bound declaration covering the entire frozen manifest, including on a historical receipt. A legacy CLI receipt lacking that evidence requires a fresh complete review.
4. Dispatch by current obligations. Include changed hunks with context, causal locations, affected callers and tests, and access to the complete frozen source. Store a causal location as a finding fingerprint plus source revision, path and anchored range. Re-anchor by verified context, expand ambiguous mappings to the whole affected boundary when its extent is verified, otherwise to the entire candidate with zero carry, and reopen newly identified causal sites even outside the delta. If consolidation first discovers an uncovered site, retain the discovery and persist INCOMPLETE until the responsible angle returns a fresh packet inspecting that exact site on the frozen candidate and consolidation reconciles it. Parent admission must independently verify that evidence. Changed-line counts order or partition work within an angle when needed. Group related producer/consumer changes together. Keep six actual angle completions and a fresh cross-file consolidation. Leave numeric thresholds and angle skipping out of this revision.
5. Implement and test through capture, native and host dispatch, retained writer, parent admission and QA in that order. Use disposable real Git repositories, the shipped scripts, and fixture reviewer responses at the model boundary. Drive the production parent admission entry point using the actual retained artifacts. Recompute eligibility at admission from trusted evidence and frozen Git objects. Apply existing bounded/path-safe reads and scope identity checks to added references. If receipt provenance or impact cannot be established before dispatch, review the entire candidate fresh. If submitted evidence fails verification, reject the result and obtain fresh evidence.
