2. **The consolidator can describe a review that the runners reject.** Step 1 accepts the union of angles' findings and reported paths. It calls a wholly missed file a non-blocker. Step 6 permits PASS-with-triage for a non-blocker. Runner 2 step 3 instead requires *each* specialist to list the full manifest. A finding mentioning a path is insufficient proof of inspection. With a one-file manifest and five of six angles listing that file, the union is complete but the native runner stops. Reproduced with `python3 -m unittest discover -s skills/shark-rider/skills/deep-review/scripts -p test_review_workflow.py -v` (one test passed, including the “empty” subcase, INCOMPLETE with zero consolidator calls). This establishes a contradictory instruction and a guarded runtime path. No successful admission of an uncovered candidate was observed.
3. **The report conflict survives template expansion, but its cause is narrower than the bug description.** The shared process embeds the portable craft workflow. Its F and G sections already require per-file quality scores and risk hotspots. Both headings appear in each checked-in shared, bug, change and feature rendered prompt. The craft workflow requires all sections, then permits a four-line report for a diff under 50 lines. The shared output policy and consolidator instead reserve compact output for complete reviews with zero findings. The process's final detailed-report list mentions findings and AC verification, omits F/G, and summarizes the verdict as PASS or FAIL. Someone following the closing list alone can omit substantive evidence or flatten PASS-with-triage. A tiny diff with a blocker exercises the conflicting exceptions. Feature review includes craft directly and adds its own tier-specific report instructions, so changing the shared partial alone misses a production caller.
