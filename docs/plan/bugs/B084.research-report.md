# B084 research report — Linked decision constraints in bug development

## Scope

Trace accepted decisions from a bug’s related-document links and explicit references through repair planning, independent plan review, and development dispatch. The bug concerns whether an applicable decision can be omitted from the developer’s checks when the approved plan and project quality gate are otherwise satisfactory. The workflow crosses the parent Shark record, worker checkout, and immutable approval snapshot, which warrants complex rigor.

A **related document** is a discoverable record with a title and path. A **reference** is an explicit pointer in the bug or its accepted evidence. An **authoritative decision** is a current, accepted source governing the repair. A **prior finding** is evidence to evaluate for applicability. The approved plan’s **coverage index** maps obligations to their authoritative sources, production boundaries, required assertions, and links to observed evidence in a separate mutable record.

### Boundary risks and options

- Look up links against the parent Shark project even when source work occurs in a nested worktree. Resolve document pointers from their owning project and use the approved plan snapshot for the approved approach. A new or changed applicable decision can invalidate coverage. A stale worker copy or an unreadable linked authority cannot establish that coverage is complete.
- A related-document title or link proves discoverability. Applicability and authority come from the source and its status. A predecessor review can identify tests and risks. Conflicting or superseded decisions need an explicit disposition, with authority conflicts escalated through the existing repair contract.
- A developer-only instruction would detect omissions late, after an independently approved plan may already exist. Sharing the same concise lookup and reconciliation rule with planning and plan review catches omissions earlier and retains the developer check for resumed work and later discoveries.
- Automatically inserting every linked document into the rendered prompt would improve visibility, but would also introduce unbounded or irrelevant inputs, cross-checkout path resolution, and authority classification problems. Hard admission based on a plan digest identifies revisions, while a correct interpretation of natural-language decisions still requires review. The existing keyed lookup plus the approved coverage index is the smaller repair. Installed binaries and project-local prompt overrides resolve their own content, so source and effective dispatch should both be checked before claiming rollout.

## Decisions

1. Extend the current bug repair workflow. Require `shark related-docs list <bug-key> --json` using the parent command context, plus inspection of explicit references in the bug, research, and approved plan. Read applicable decisions and prior findings. In repair planning, map every applicable constraint to its source, production caller or boundary, and a testable assertion or justified disposition in the existing coverage index. In plan review, check completeness against the sources independently.
2. In development, apply the check to new, existing, and resumed fixes. Record actual assertions and results in the linked evidence record and preserve the approved snapshot. Permit `pass` only when the existing gates and applicable decision obligations are evidenced or explicitly dispositioned by the appropriate authority. Route omitted or changed obligations that require a material plan update to `replan`, stale approval to `review_plan`, changed diagnosis to `needs_research`, and unavailable required external evidence to `blocked`. Reuse the parent-owned transitions and existing gate envelope.
3. Verify the rendered bug instructions through keyed `next` and the in-process `run` path. Exercise a decision discoverable only through a link, an explicit reference without a link, a no-decision control, and a resumed fix with green tests whose plan omits an applicable decision. Require the latter to route through replanning and retain existing behavior for the no-decision case. Use the existing prompt golden and B036 integration harness, with a counterfactual that leaves the nominal regression and quality gate green.
