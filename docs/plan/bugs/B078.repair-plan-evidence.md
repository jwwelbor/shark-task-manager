### Caller cancellation

B078/3853, `B078-plan-r3#F3#caller-cancellation`. The shipped `Unlink` passes its caller’s context to `ExecContext`. The project requires propagating cancellation, and `dbconn.DB.BeginTxContext` already delegates to `sql.DB.BeginTx(ctx, nil)`. In the independent review the pre-cancelled call returned an error matching `context.Canceled` and a fresh reader counted one association. A live-context retry succeeded and the count became zero. Row 15 retains this direct test, checks propagation at the new transaction boundaries, and requires a deterministic rollback check after DELETE. The latter checks are candidate obligations.
