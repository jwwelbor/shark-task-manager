### Findings — the historical scope used to classify a new sibling

E34-F06 REQ-F-005 and its reviewed TC-006 define how a successor reviewer classifies a new fingerprint. A finding of the same class inside a previously completed sweep's search scope is a recurrence. A finding outside that scope, or inside it under a different class, takes the ordinary finding route. The shipped bug code-review prompt applies that policy on re-review. The class workflow also preserves owner-accepted risk on an unchanged fingerprint and records material conflicts for owner resolution. B087 decision 4185 requires fresh inspection after rejection. Fresh inspection locates the sibling but cannot establish what the earlier sweep covered. [Feature specification](../E34-prompt-and-skill-improvements/E34-F06-defect-class-completeness-and-recurrence-routing/spec.md) and [reviewed scenarios TC-006, TC-007 and TC-012](../E34-prompt-and-skill-improvements/E34-F06-defect-class-completeness-and-recurrence-routing/scenario-review-TC-005-TC-009.md).
