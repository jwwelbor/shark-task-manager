## Use these five dimensions

| Code | What you assess |
|---|---|
| P | Is the problem clear, and are the intended users and scope specific? |
| M | Can success be observed or measured, and are constraints stated? |
| R | Are research claims relevant and supported by the supplied references? |
| F | Is the approach feasible, with reasons for its trade-offs? |
| S | Does the final synthesis and decomposition agree with the earlier scope? |

Use whole-number grades: **0** absent or contradicted; **1** major gaps;
**2** usable direction with material gaps; **3** mostly clear and supported;
**4** clear, specific, supported, and consistent. These correspond to rubric
scores `0`, `0.25`, `0.50`, `0.75`, and `1.00`.

Do not give an overall grade separately. The overall label is the sum of the
five grades divided by 20. Briefly explain each grade; one sentence can cover
multiple dimensions when it identifies the relevant evidence.
