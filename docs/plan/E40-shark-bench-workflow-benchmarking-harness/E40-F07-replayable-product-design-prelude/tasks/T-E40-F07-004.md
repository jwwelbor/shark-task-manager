# Task: Live-egress denial set, run-prelude.sh scaffold, and structural/observational proof

## Goal
Stand up the closed live-egress set, `run-prelude.sh`'s PATH-resolved dispatch scaffold with its
structural denial-argument construction, and `verify-replay-isolation.sh`'s observational transcript scan
— proving both halves of "a scored run cannot reach live research or an interactive question surface"
independently.
