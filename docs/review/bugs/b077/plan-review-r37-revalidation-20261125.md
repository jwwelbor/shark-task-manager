**C27, resolving and delivering.** Rider carries the final responder’s session through configured-owner resolution. The service compares it to the persisted last response. The write checks the live claim and TTL alongside the expected state. C27 interleaves takeover, expiry or release between destination validation and the write, with the owner, accepted answers, destination and asking lease fixed. An early heartbeat alone permits a stale resolution. The required observation is unchanged Question state, zero new notes/history or answer deliveries and an intact successor lease. The live control resolves once, releases the precise session, and delivers the bounded answer and evidence from the owner read. Direct owner resolution without a Rider session keeps its existing behavior. The candidate also contains a regression for a successor supplying its own live session for the same responder. This case distinguishes current claim ownership from authorship of the final response.
