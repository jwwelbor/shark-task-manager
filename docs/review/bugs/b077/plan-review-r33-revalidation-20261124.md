## Open blocker B077-R42-01 — a receipt from a different review can satisfy C12

The companion CLI report writer accepts the review body, candidate-bound decisions and retained attempt array as separate inputs. Its receipt reader requires one accepted entry and populated identity fields. The writer checks that the winner names the expected CLI, copies its observed model, usage and cost, and validates the body and decisions against the candidate through a separate path. Those checks have no common identifier for the packet that the accepted invocation produced. The existing positive writer fixture supplies a hand-built receipt alongside a separately constructed body. This finding follows from the caller trace. It makes no claim that an operator has actually mixed up two reports. Its wrong-ID and missing-observation cases can reject malformed or internally inconsistent receipts. Its two-attempt control can catch copying a winner’s model onto a losing invocation. All of those checks can succeed when an independently valid receipt for review B is attached to the body and decisions of review A. Both reviews can share the candidate, CLI, provider, model, verdict and completion counts. Each receipt can contain a real worker and the correct native charge for its own invocation. A’s published report would then credit B’s worker and charge. C05 tests the outer adapter’s producing turn and C25 tests the source consumed by reviewers. Both can pass in this case.
